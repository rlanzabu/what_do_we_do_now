Install Your Font

Create an installable TrueType font file (.ttf) from your saved characters and use it on your computer in other applications.

Preview
Aa Bb 123 ÄÖÜ äöü ß

Your font preview will appear after the font file is generated. Missing characters will be listed during export. OpenType.js is loaded from a CDN, so an internet connection is required for TTF export.

Installation:
Windows: Download the .ttf file, open it, and select “Install”.
macOS: Open the .ttf file with Font Book and click “Install”.
You may need to restart other applications.
