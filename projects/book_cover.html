Paint and play
Choose Add shape and click to place it. Use Select / move to drag it. Select a shape to change its size, colour, or opacity. New shapes start at 180 px.
Pattern
Draw a motif below with Pen, Line, Brush, Spray, or Add shape. This pad is transparent. Its shapes become part of the motif when placed. Repeats the pad across the illustration area, below text. Change size or spacing, then Undo and reapply to try another arrangement.
Finish

Canvas: 1080 × 1350 pixels. Export your book cover before closing or refreshing the page. Clear Canvas removes all drawings, shapes, patterns, images, and text, and restores a white background. Margin guides and selection outlines are not exported.
